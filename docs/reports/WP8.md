## Decisions taken where the brief and the design were silent (or differ)
- **Batch number shape**: the design's and WP7a's `DOA-ALL-ALL-LS-000001` (dashes, ALL for All, from `cycle.batchCode`), not the codes run together as the brief writes them; the same choice WP7a made.
- **A future board date**: the brief says schedule it; the design's demo refuses a future board date. I followed the brief: a future date is scheduled (at most a year ahead, as WEF). Upload Advanced Rates ("past advancements") refuses a future effective date.
- **Time in rate** counts from the latest `rating_advancement` line of the current rate, else `seniority_date`, else `date_employed`. Advancing does not change `seniority_date`.
- **Add Personnel** accepts a rating not yet due (the old page did) but not one in the top rate; a department-tied user may add only their department's ratings.
- **Rate changed under a proposal** (by upload or the record): Set New Rate and the board date refuse it with the reason ("now LS, no longer AB"); a scheduled change that can no longer be made is cancelled and the row waits for a date again.
- **Upload duplicates** are the same rating, new rate and date; a later promotion of the same rating is allowed. The upload refuses a rating with an unfinished advancement (batch or scheduled change). Rates may be written by code or name. Its remark is kept in `advancement_uploads`.
- **Report filters**: Branch matches the batch's branch or any of its ratings' branch (a batch for All branches would otherwise never match); Rate matches the batch's rate or any row's old or new rate.
- Close is `edit` on `advancement.close`, the board date `edit` on `advancement.board`, the import `add` on `advancement.upload` (the brief names only approve on Approval).
