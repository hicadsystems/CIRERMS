## Built (against the brief, page by page)
- **Tables** (migration `20261120_070000_create_drafting.sql`, block 07): `batches` (flow, number, code, six-digit sequence per code, the filters, status `open`/`approved`/`closed`, initiated by and when, closed by and when, authority (unique), close date), `draft_rows` (rating, rate and specialisation, current ship, date joined, months there, proposed ship, vice or deployment, remark, request, Not Recommended with reason, by and when, the department's approval, WEF, status `proposed`/`not_recommended`/`awaiting_wef`/`scheduled`/`reported`, the posting it opened, its scheduled change), `batch_approvals` (per flow and department), `manning_requirements` (ship, rate, specialisation, quantity, unique triple), `settings` (key, value, description) and `scheduled_changes` (kind, rating, effective date, payload, status, source). A foreign key for every link (Static Tables' in-use check sees ships, rates, branches and specialisations used by drafting). No procedures, functions, triggers or views.
- **One posting move** (`src/modules/drafting/postings.service.js`): `move(tx, actor, { serviceNumber, shipId, wef, authority, remarks, source })`. A WEF today or earlier ends the open posting the day before and opens the new one on the WEF (`rating_drafting`); the record's Drafting tab shows it and the current ship follows. A WEF after today is **Scheduled** (`scheduled_changes`, kind `posting`); the record's Drafting tab shows "Drafted to X wef dd/mm/yyyy" with a Scheduled pill (`public/js/sections/drafting-record.js`); the server makes the move on that date. Refused: a former rating, an unknown ship, the ship the rating is on, a WEF on or before the start of the current posting.
- **Scheduled moves: the mechanism chosen is a task in the server** (`src/shared/scheduled`): at start (after 5 s) and then every hour, the server applies every change due on or before today (server local date), oldest first, each in its own transaction through the handler registered for its kind; so a move is made at most an hour after midnight of its date, or at the next start if the server was down. A handler's AppError (the change can no longer be made) cancels it with the reason (the draft row goes back to waiting for a WEF); any other error leaves it for the next run. Audited as `SYSTEM`. Chosen over "apply when read" because current ship is read in many places (ratings lists, record, dashboard, reports) that would each need the hook.
- **Update Manning Requirement**: filters Ship, Rate, Specialisation; three figures (requirements, required, aboard); list of Ship, Rate and Specialisation, Required, Aboard (current postings of that ship, rate and specialisation), Difference (Full / Short by n / Over by n); a row opens its record with Edit and Delete (confirmed); Add Requirement; duplicate refused (naming the ship, rate and specialisation), quantity 1 to 99; **Print** (one ship or all) opens the Manning Requirement Report (ships as groups, totals) in the app's preview. Advisory only.
- **Print Draft Proposal**: Branch, Specialisation, Rate (each "All"); the due list (reads only; 25 a page from the server) with Official No, Name (rate, names, sex, "in <batch>"), Current Ship, On Ship (months pill); a row opens the rating (DOJ ship, last ship 1 and 2, authority of the draft that brought them there, batch) with Go to Review and Open Record. **Create Draft Batch** opens a confirming dialog (batch number, filters, ratings to add, how many left out, whether it adds to an open batch); one rating is enough; nothing is written before Confirm.
- **Review/Print Draft**: open batches in a picker; batch facts and progress ("n of m have a proposed ship"); list Official No, Name (current ship), On Ship, Proposed Ship (with the **Over by n / Under by n** pill against the manning requirement, counting what all open batches would bring and take away; advisory). A row's dialog: **Edit Posting** (proposed ship, the current one left out and refused; vice or deployment; remark), **Not Recommended** (required reason; struck out in the list, on the Draft Form and in the workbook; back on the due list), **Remove** (confirmed), Open Record. **Add Personnel** with the rating search select and Request (Medical, Special Requests, Others), refusing a rating in a batch (naming it), a former rating, one with no current posting. **Print Draft** (the Draft Form, landscape, not-recommended lines struck), **Export Excel**, **Clear Draft** (confirmed).
- **Draft Approval**: one row per department (specialisation) over the open batches: Department, Total, Status, Approved By; figures (departments, approved, waiting); the dialog shows the totals by rate group (every rate from the Rates table in its order; nothing hard-coded), the department's ratings, and **Approve Department** (rank, name, appointment; the *approve* privilege), refused while a rating has no proposed ship (button off, notice, and 409 from the API); approving again warns it replaces the earlier approval. **Export Excel** for the department and **Export Excel All Departments** (the demo shows both).
- **Close Draft**: open batches with Ratings and Ready; a row's dialog with its readiness; **Close Draft** (Authority, Close Date dd/mm/yyyy) only when every rating has a proposed ship and every department is approved; authority new (names the batch that used it), date not future, not before the batch began. Final (409 on any later change). After closing, the page moves to Update WEF on that batch.
- **Update WEF**: closed batches with an authority, still waiting or closed in the last 30 days; Department filter; batch facts with "Dated n of m"; a row's **Set WEF**; **Set WEF for Department** (only that batch's pending ratings with a proposed ship, the count shown); WEF not before the authority date, at most a year ahead; saving calls the posting move (reported now, or scheduled).

## Decisions taken where the brief and the design were silent
- **Batch number shape**: the design's `DOD-SM-ALL-ALL-000006` (dashes between the codes, `ALL` for All), which the brief's "Common to both" points to; the brief's item 4 writes the codes run together. The counter is per code, over open and closed batches.
- **"In a batch"** also covers a closed batch's rating still waiting for a WEF or scheduled, so a rating is never drafted twice.
- **A department's batches** (for a department-tied user) are the batches made for that specialisation; a batch for "All" belongs to no single department.
- **Approval** covers the department's live rows in every open batch (as the old page); changing a row's proposed ship after approval takes the approval back for that row (the department shows Waiting again); adding a rating does the same for the new row. A batch's `approved` status is worked out after every change.
- **Close authority** is unique across every batch (drafting and, later, advancement), as the old procedure refused a reused signal.
- **WEF limit** of one year ahead (the demo's rule) is a constant, not a setting.
- **Who-did-it links** to `users` are `ON DELETE SET NULL` (accounts are deactivated, never deleted; the system-admin walkthrough deletes the accounts it does not know).
- The close privilege is `edit` on `drafting.close` and the WEF privilege `edit` on `drafting.wef` (the brief names only approve on Draft Approval).

## Not verified
- MariaDB: not run (the SQL avoids MySQL-only syntax as far as I know; `JSON` and `TIMESTAMPDIFF` are used).
- The hourly timer itself was not left running past a midnight; its function (`applyDue`) was run with a future date, and the server's start-up run was seen to apply nothing when nothing was due.
- Two people making the same batch at the same moment: rows are locked (`FOR UPDATE`) and the batch number is unique, but no concurrent test was run.
- The dates use the server's local day (and the database's `NOW()` for timestamps); a server and database in different time zones near midnight were not tried.
