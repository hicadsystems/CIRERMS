## Built (against the brief, page by page)
Everything the first build set out to do runs now, against MySQL 8 (Docker), with the preinstalled Chromium for the PDF.

- **Process Training Draft**: the figures (open batches, ratings proposed, courses), the open batches, a row opens the batch as label and value rows with *Open in Review* and *Add Ratings*; **New Training Batch** in two steps (course and period, then Branch, Specialisation and Rate with the live count of eligible ratings), a duplicate refused naming its batch, Create Batch disabled when nobody matches, the new batch opened in Review.
- **Training Review**: the batch picker, the batch in a card (two panels of label and value rows), the ratings (Official No, name with rate and length of service, last course or *First course*), **Add Rating** with the rating search select (every refusal under the field), **Add by Branch**, **Remove** after a confirmation.
- **Update Training History**: the Attended box on each row (on each card on a phone), a row opens its dialog with **Change Dates**, **Confirm Training History** (institution, the batch dates, a result for each rating that attended); confirming adds the Training entries (with the result in `classification`) and closes the batch. The rating's record shows the new entries on its Training tab (looked at).
- **Reports**: the four filters, the batch list, a batch's dialog with its ratings and results, **Print PDF** (the Training Draft Report in the app's preview; a 63-rating batch runs to three pages, the footer on each).
- **Excel Upload**: Download Template, Choose File, Review, Import.
- **Privileges and seed**: unchanged in design (`training.process`, `review`, `history`, `reports`, `upload`); the Training and Training Viewer accounts now exist (see the bugs).

## Bugs found by running it, and how they were fixed
The migration, the other seed statements, the eligibility fragment, the bulk `INSERT ... SELECT`, the last-course subqueries, the Excel template and upload and the PDF through `renderPdf` ran correctly as written. What failed:
1. **The two demo accounts did not exist.** Seed 007 made accounts for `B0562` and `B0541`, which already belong to other accounts (a deactivated Rating and the Records Clerk), so `INSERT IGNORE` skipped them silently and the Training and Training Viewer roles had nobody. They now have ratings of their own made in the seed, `B0901` (Training) and `B0902` (Training Viewer), as the drafting seed does for its accounts. README's account table lists them.
2. **New Training Batch could never be made from the screen.** The dialog read the original `<select>`, which the dropdown component replaces when the dialog opens, so *Next* always said "Choose the course." and the course's rule never showed. The dialog now reads the field through the form. The same fault made the **batch picker** in Review and History ignore a change; it now listens as the drafting picker does.
3. **The Training Draft Report footer read "Generated by: [object Object]"** (the actor was passed whole). It now names the person, as disciplinary does.
4. **The PDF's Rate column** printed the full name over three lines; it is the short code now, as in the design sample.
5. **The Attended checkbox** was the browser's blue, not the colour token (`accent-primary`; CSS rebuilt).
6. **Add Rating** showed its refusal twice (a notice and the field message); it shows it once, under the field.
7. **Step 2 of New Training Batch** ended with *Create Batch* and Next's arrow; it has the check icon now.
8. **A field's message stayed** after the person changed the field; it goes on change (also the results in Confirm).
9. **The figures' labels and the batch picker** were cut off on a phone; the labels wrap, the picker shows the batch number and course.
10. **The old walkthrough** counted the API refusals it made on purpose as browser errors (400) and drew the pages without driving one action; the Training role's sign-in timed out because of (1).
11. **Static Tables walkthrough.** Courses are in use now (entries and batches), so its "no record refers to this table yet" check for Training failed in four layouts. It declares the holder (`rating_training.training_course_id`), as WP4 did for offences. One line.

## Decisions taken where the brief and the design were silent
All the first build's decisions stand (batch number from the course id; the result in `classification` and on the batch row; both branch and specialisation must match when a course sets both; a duplicate is refused whatever the other batch's status; the Authority upload column is kept in the audit row; update uses PUT). Added in this pass:
- **Demo accounts** have ratings of their own (`B0901`, `B0902`) rather than taking over ratings of the first demo set: taking `B0614` or `B0627` would break the system-admin walkthrough, which makes accounts for them.
- **Reports keeps a dialog** for a batch's contents, as the brief and the Wide-tables rule say. The design demo opens a separate page (`training/reports/<no>`) with *Export Excel*; I did not build that.
- **The facts of a batch** are two panels of label and value rows (as the drafting batch card), not the demo's small-capital label strip, which the README's rules forbid.
- **Names** are written as in the demo: rate abbreviation, then the name; the PDF uses the surname in capitals and the short rate, as the sample does.
- **The Process page's figures** (open batches, ratings proposed, courses) are counted from every open batch, not only the page on show.
- **Reviewing a long batch**: the walkthrough makes a batch of every eligible rating for ICT Fundamentals (February 2024) to see the report run to a second page; the demo database keeps no such batch.
