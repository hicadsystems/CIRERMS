header buttons, filter labels, figures and columns; then I looked at every page at 1440 px light and 390 px dark (about 134
screenshots) and at each main dialog's fit.

**Fixed**: the dashboard's tiles; the Memos and Mails tile; the two Training pages starting empty; the label breaks; mixed-case names.

**Left, with the reason** (the rules that win over the demo, or an earlier decision):
- Reports (WP9).
- Ratings lists: the demo's four header buttons (Copy Rows, Excel, PDF, CSV) are one *Export* menu with the same four items.
- Manage Users: *Add Account* (not "Add User": it starts by finding the rating, PLAN 9d); Locked, Created, Failed Attempts, Last Sign In
  and Linked Rating are in the row's dialog, not table columns (wide-table rule).
- Manage Roles: the roles are a list and the privilege grid is in the Add/Edit dialog (wide-table rule); no "Save Changes" button.
- Manage Password: the demo's page changes your own password; the app's resets someone else's by official number (PLAN 9d), and
  Change Password is in the account menu.
- Audit Trail: IP and Changes are in the row's dialog (wide-table rule).
- Training: the pages are titled *Training Reports* and *Training Excel Upload* (the tabs read *Reports* and *Excel Upload* as in the
  demo) so they are not mistaken for the Reports module; a batch opens in a dialog, not a page with Export Excel (WP6's question).
- Close Draft and Close (advancement) show a Branch column the demo lacks; Concluded Cases and Disciplinary Report have a Sanction
  filter the demo lacks; Rate DOD History has Export Excel (the owner's follow-up).
- Subtitles use curly apostrophes where the demo has straight ones, and a few subtitles read longer than the demo's.
- Module tab strips are untouched: on a screen too narrow for all of Drafting's nine or Static Tables' sixteen tabs they scroll, as
  before (the owner, 23/11/2026: WP10 does not alter how the tabs work; a round-1 change to wrap them was undone in round 1b).

## Decisions taken where the brief and the design were silent
- **Relatives' names** (next of kin, spouse, children) stay as typed ("Adaeze Chioma Okonkwo"): the app does not capitalise them and
  the demo shows them in normal case. Only ratings' names are capitals. If the owner wants them in capitals everywhere, the seed
  and the forms both change.
- **Dashboard counts**: "reported this year" counts drafting rows with status reported and a WEF in the current year (the batch
  cycle only; special drafts are in Special Drafting); "open batches" are drafting batches open or approved, not closed;
  "proposed" is the discharge rows still pending (a scheduled discharge is not counted as a proposal, and counts as a discharge
  only once closed).
- **The dashboard needs `dashboard.home`**: a role without it (for example Training Viewer) gets a 403 from the numbers, as before.
- A rating's user name is changed in `ratings.service.updateRating`, not by a database trigger (no triggers, PLAN rule 10).
