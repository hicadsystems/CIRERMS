## Built (against the brief, page by page)
- **Special Draft Proposal** (`#special-drafting/proposal`): the drafts not yet printed (25 a page; Search by official number or name; Posting Type filter; a count). A row opens the draft's record (rating, current posting and months there, the draft, its status) with **Draft Letter** (the one-rating printout; marks nothing), **Open Record** and **Remove** (confirmed; only before printing). **Draft a Rating** finds any current rating with the rating search (no batch number), shows its rate, branch, current ship, date joined and months there, and takes Posting Type, Drafted To, Authority, WEF, Deployment/Vice and Remark. A rating that already has an unfinished draft is told so before the form is filled.
- **Special Draft Report** (`#special-drafting/report`): the posting types with the count waiting, the waiting drafts of the chosen type, **Preview Draft Form** (a PDF that marks nothing), **Print and Mark Printed** (confirmed: one numbered form, every waiting draft of the type is marked printed and each rating moves on its WEF through WP7a's posting move; a WEF after today is scheduled and made on its date by the server), the **Printed Forms** list and **Reprint Form**.
- **Upload Draft** (`#special-drafting/upload`): Download Template, Choose File (every row checked, nothing written), Review and Import (one transaction), Download Error Rows. The old Draft6 sheet is refused with a message that says so.
- Printouts: D4's `special-draft-form` and `special-draft-letter` templates.
- Navigation: Special Drafting is no longer marked Soon.

## Decisions taken where the brief and the design were silent (or differ)
1. **Authority is required** on every draft (the old page took it as the signal that authorises the draft; the form prints it). Easy to relax in `problemsWith` (`special-drafting.service.js`).
2. **Posting types are fixed codes** in the service (`POSTING_TYPES`: Complement, Vice, TY, DW). The meaning of TY and DW is not known (PLAN 9e). They are not a Static Table; if Hicad wants them editable they become one.
3. **Privileges:** `special-drafting.proposal` view, add (Draft a Rating), delete (Remove); `special-drafting.report` view (report, preview, Printed Forms, Reprint), edit (Print and Mark Printed); `special-drafting.upload` view (template), add (check and import). The brief names add and edit; delete for Remove follows the design's D3 privilege map.
4. **Two roles and accounts:** *Special Drafting* (`B1101`: proposes, removes, uploads, reads the report) and *Special Drafting Officer* (`B1102`: also prints). Printing writes postings, so it is its own privilege.
5. **A draft is final once printed.** Remove works only on an unprinted draft (409 otherwise). Corrections after printing go on the rating's record and are in the audit trail.
6. **One unfinished draft per rating.** A rating with a draft waiting, or printed with its move still scheduled, cannot get another (checked under a lock on the rating's row; the upload skips such rows).
7. **Print is all or nothing.** Before numbering the form, every draft is checked with WP7a's own move check; if any cannot be moved now (for example the rating has meanwhile moved to that ship) nothing is printed, the message names the ratings, and the Report page says why and keeps Print off. The fix is to Remove them on Proposal.
8. **WEF rules:** after the day the rating joined its current ship and no more than 365 days ahead (as Drafting's Update WEF). A WEF today or earlier moves the rating when the form is printed; a later one is scheduled (scheduled change kind `posting`, source `special_drafts`), and the draft becomes *Moved* (or *Cancelled*, with the reason) when the server applies or cannot apply it.
9. **Draft statuses:** `unprinted`, `scheduled`, `moved`, `cancelled`. The rating's rate, ship, date joined and months are kept when the draft is proposed and refreshed at printing, so a reprint shows the form as it was.
10. **Form numbers** are `SDF-YYYY-NNNN`, counted per year (unique key on year and sequence). Draft letters are referenced `SDL-YYYY-NNNN` (the draft's id).
11. **Print and Mark Printed is a separate, confirmed button**, not in the preview's footer as the design shows: the shared PDF preview (`ui/pdf-preview.js`) has no footer action and I did not change a shared file for it. After printing, the printed form opens in the preview.
12. **Special drafts are independent of DOD batches.** A rating in an open drafting batch can also have a special draft; nothing links the two. If the owner wants that blocked, it goes in `createDraft` and `problemsOfDrafts`.
13. **Upload columns:** Official No, Drafted To (ship name as in Static Tables; inactive ships refused), Authority, WEF (dd/mm/yyyy or a date cell), Posting Type (any case), Deployment/Vice, Remark. Rows with errors are listed and not imported; a rating with an unfinished draft, or in the file twice (second row), is skipped. Capped at 2000 rows.
14. **Audit:** each proposal, removal and import row (`special_drafts`), each form (`special_draft_forms`) and each printed or scheduled-then-applied draft is in the audit trail with the actor.
