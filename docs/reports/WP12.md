## Built (against the brief, page by page)
- **Recommendation and Grading** (`#assessment/grading`): filters (rating search select, Period, Ship, Rate, Grade matching either grade), 25 a page. **Initiate Appraisal** for a current rating and an open period: Character and Efficiency grades from Appraisal Ratings, remark, CO's name (required), director's name and date (optional, together; not in the future, not before the period began). The rate and ship at the time are stored. One active appraisal per rating per period (refused naming the existing one). **Edit** while the period is open (nothing changed is refused; the changes go to the audit trail with before and after, and the appraisal's dialog lists them). **Cancel Appraisal** with a required reason; it leaves the lists and the rating may be appraised again.
- **Report on Appraisal** (`#assessment/report`): filters Period, Ship, Rate, Branch, Grade; three figures (appraisals, Character A or B, Efficiency A or B); grade counts for both grades; the list; **Print PDF** (D4's `report-on-appraisal` template) and **Export Excel**, all from one query (`reportRows`).
- **Appraisal Periods** (`#assessment/periods`): counts, list, Add Period, Edit, **Close Period** (confirmed, final; warns how many current ratings have no appraisal). Names unique, end after start, no overlap.
- **The record**: the rating's appraisals as a list under the Advancement tab, only for whoever may view Recommendation and Grading; not on My Details; not in the completeness. Initiate Appraisal from there when a period is open.
- **Dashboard**: the Appraisals tile counts appraisals recorded this month and the open periods.
- Privileges: `appraisal.periods` view/add/edit, `appraisal.grading` view/add/edit/delete, `appraisal.report` view. Roles Naval Assessment (`B1201`) and Naval Assessment Officer (`B1202`) in seed `011_appraisal.sql`, with demo periods and appraisals (one edited, one cancelled). Naval Character Assessment loses Soon; Ctrl K reads the nav catalogue.
- Migration `20261120_120000_create_appraisal.sql`; no procedures, functions, triggers or views.

## Decisions taken where the brief and the design were silent
1. **Edit, Close Period and Edit Period are the `edit` action; Cancel is `delete`**, as the design's privilege map says.
2. **One active appraisal per rating and period** uses `appraisals.active_slot` (1 or NULL) under a unique key, so cancelled rows never block a new one; the service also checks first under a lock on the rating's row.
3. **An appraisal has a number** `APR-NNNN` (its id), as the design shows.
4. **Changing a period's start** is refused when it would pass an existing director's date. Closed periods cannot be edited.
5. **Only current ratings** are appraised; grades that became inactive stay on appraisals that hold them but cannot be newly chosen.
6. **Edit history** is read back from the audit trail (`audit_trail`, table `appraisals`), not a separate table.
7. **The tabs follow the demo's order** (grading, report, periods); routes `assessment/*`, screens `appraisal.*`.
8. **View Appraisals** on a period opens grading with that period fixed (`?period=<id>`), with a notice to show all periods.
