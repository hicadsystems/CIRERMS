CIRERMS is the Nigerian Navy's ratings records system. This chapter is for anyone who has never used it.
It shows you how to sign in, find your way around and use this guide.

You see only the screens your role may use. Your screen may show fewer items than the pictures here.

## Signing In {#getting-started-signing-in}

Your user name is your Official Number. An administrator gives you a password the first time.

![The sign-in page](shot:getting-started-sign-in-page)

### Sign in {#getting-started-sign-in}

1. Open CIRERMS in your browser. The sign-in page opens.
2. Type your Official Number in **Username or Official Number**.
3. Type your password in **Password**. Press the eye at the right of the box to see what you typed.
4. Press **Sign In**.
5. The Dashboard opens, or the first screen your role may use.

> **Tip:** Type your Official Number as it is on your record, for example `B0539`. Capital letters are filled in for you.

> **Important:** Never share your password, and never write it where others can read it. Sign out when you leave your desk.

If the page says the user name or password is wrong, check both and try again. After many wrong tries it asks you to wait 15 minutes.

### Forgot your password {#getting-started-forgot-password}

If you cannot remember your password, CIRERMS can e-mail you a code. The code works for 10 minutes and only once.

1. On the sign-in page, press **Forgot password?** above the password box.
2. Type your Official Number in **Official Number**.
3. Press **Send Code**.
4. Read the message on the page. It says the same for every Official Number, so it does not tell you whether the e-mail was sent. Open your e-mail and find the message from CIRERMS.
5. Type the 6 digits of the code in **Code**.
6. Type your new password in **New Password** and again in **Confirm New Password**. The box shows each rule as you type.
7. Press **Reset Password**.
8. The sign-in page opens with a message. Sign in with your new password.

![The reset step](shot:getting-started-reset-password)

If the page says **That code is not right, or it has expired**, check the 6 digits. A code stops working after 5 wrong tries. Press **Send a new code** to get another one. You can ask for 3 codes in an hour.

No e-mail on your account? Ask your administrator to reset your password. The administrator gives you a one-time password, and you choose your own at your next sign-in.

> **Important:** When your password is reset, every place you were signed in is signed out. If you did not ask for a code, ignore the e-mail. Your password has not changed.

### Choose your own password {#getting-started-first-password}

A new account, or one that an administrator reset, has a one-time password. It works once.

1. Sign in with the password you were given.
2. The **Choose a New Password** box opens before anything else.
3. Type the one-time password in **Current Password**.
4. Type your own password in **New Password** and again in **Confirm New Password**.
5. Press **Set Password**.

Your password needs at least 8 characters, a letter and a number. It must be different from the one you had.
The box shows each rule as you type.

### Sign out {#getting-started-sign-out}

1. Open the account menu. It is at the bottom of the sidebar, or at the top right on a phone.
2. Press **Sign Out**.

## The Layout {#getting-started-layout}

Every screen has the same parts, so once you know them you can find your way anywhere.

### On a desktop {#getting-started-layout-desktop-task}

1. The sidebar is at the left. It lists the modules, such as **Ratings Management** and **Drafting**. Press a name to open it.
2. The module tabs are under the top bar. Each tab is one page of the module. Press a tab to open that page.
3. The search bar is at the top. It finds screens and ratings. See *Searching* below.
4. The account button is at the bottom of the sidebar. It shows your Official Number and your role.
5. Press the button at the top left, next to the search bar, to fold the sidebar to icons. Press it again to open it.

![The layout on a desktop](shot:getting-started-layout-desktop)

### On a phone {#getting-started-layout-phone-task}

1. The header is at the top. It shows the page title, a magnifier for search and your account.
2. Under the header, a row of chips lists the pages of the module. Swipe the row to see more.
3. The bar at the bottom has **Home**, **Ratings**, **Documents**, **My Details** and **More**. You see only the items your role may open.
4. Press **More** to see every module you may use, as tiles. Press a tile to open that module.

![The layout on a phone](shot:getting-started-layout-phone)

![The More sheet on a phone](shot:getting-started-more-sheet)

> **Tip:** On a phone, a record opens with a back arrow at the top left. Press it to return to the list.

## Searching {#getting-started-search}

The search finds any screen you may open. If you may open the ratings lists, it also finds a rating by Official Number or name.

### Search for a screen or a rating {#getting-started-search-find}

1. On a desktop, hold `Ctrl` and press `K`. On a phone, press the magnifier at the top.
2. Type a few letters of what you want, for example `reports`.
3. {needs="ratings.list view, ratings.edit view"} To find a rating, type its Official Number or any part of its name.
4. Press the arrow keys to move down the list, then press `Enter`. You can also press a result.
5. Press `Esc` to close the search without choosing.

![The search open on a screen name](shot:getting-started-search)

## The Account Menu {#getting-started-account-menu}

Press the account button to open the menu. It holds everything that belongs to you.

![The account menu](shot:getting-started-account-menu)

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| {needs="ratings.my-details view"} **View My Details** | Opens your own service record. You can read it but not change it here. |
| **Change Password** | Lets you choose a new password. |
| **Lock Screen** | Hides your work until you type your password. |
| **Dark Mode** | Changes the colours for dim light. The item then reads **Light Mode**. |
| **Install App** | Installs CIRERMS on this device. It shows only when your browser offers it, and not once CIRERMS is installed. See [Installing CIRERMS](#getting-started-installing). |
| **User Guide** | Opens this guide. |
| **Sign Out** | Ends your session. |

### Change your password {#getting-started-change-password}

1. Open the account menu and press **Change Password**.
2. Type your present password in **Current Password**.
3. Type your new password in **New Password** and again in **Confirm New Password**.
4. Press **Change Password**.
5. The message **Password changed.** shows. Your other sessions are signed out.

If the box says **The passwords do not match.**, type the new password again in **Confirm New Password**.

### Switch between light and dark {#getting-started-dark-mode}

1. Open the account menu.
2. Press **Dark Mode**. The screen changes at once.
3. To go back, open the account menu and press **Light Mode**.

On a desktop, the moon button at the top right does the same. CIRERMS remembers your choice on this device.

## Installing CIRERMS {#getting-started-installing}

You can install CIRERMS on your computer or phone. It then opens in its own window, with the crest as its icon, from the Start menu, the desktop or the home screen. Installing needs no app store and no installer file. CIRERMS still works through the server, so you need a network connection, and nothing about any rating is kept on your device. You sign in as you always do.

> **Note:** Installing works only when the address of CIRERMS starts with `https://`. Your administrator sets this up. If your browser never offers to install, ask your administrator.

### Install on a computer {#getting-started-install-computer}

1. Open CIRERMS in Chrome or Edge and sign in.
2. Press the install icon at the right end of the address bar. Or open the account menu and press **Install App**.
3. Press **Install** in the box the browser shows.
4. CIRERMS opens in its own window. Next time, open it from the Start menu or the desktop.

### Install on an Android phone {#getting-started-install-android}

1. Open CIRERMS in Chrome and sign in.
2. Open the account menu at the top right and press **Install App**. Or open the browser menu and press **Install app**.
3. Press **Install**.
4. CIRERMS appears on your home screen with the crest. Press it to open.

### Install on an iPhone or iPad {#getting-started-install-iphone}

An iPhone or iPad does not offer **Install App**, so you add CIRERMS yourself. Use Safari.

1. Open CIRERMS in Safari and sign in.
2. Press **Share**, the square with an arrow.
3. Scroll down and press **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Press **Add**.
5. CIRERMS appears on your home screen with the crest. Press it to open.

### When a new version is ready {#getting-started-install-update}

CIRERMS updates itself with the server. When a new version is ready, a message at the bottom of the screen says **A new version of CIRERMS is ready.**

1. Finish what you are typing, and save it.
2. Press **Reload**.
3. CIRERMS reloads and opens the new version.

CIRERMS never reloads by itself, so you never lose what you are typing. If you ignore the message, you carry on with the old version until you press **Reload** or close and open CIRERMS again.

> **Tip:** If your network is down, CIRERMS shows **You Are Offline**. Check your network and press **Try Again**.

## The Lock Screen {#getting-started-lock-screen}

CIRERMS locks your screen after 15 minutes without use, so nobody reads your work while you are away.
The administrator can set a different time. The work you had open is still there when you unlock.

![The lock screen](shot:getting-started-lock-screen)

### Lock your screen yourself {#getting-started-lock}

1. Open the account menu.
2. Press **Lock Screen**.

### Unlock your screen {#getting-started-unlock}

1. Type your password in **Password**.
2. Press **Unlock**.

> **Important:** Only your password unlocks the screen. Press **Sign Out** on the lock screen to end your session instead.

## Previewing and Printing {#getting-started-pdf-preview}

Lists and reports open as a PDF in a preview, on the same screen. You never leave CIRERMS to read them.

![A PDF in the preview](shot:getting-started-pdf-preview)

### Download or print a document {#getting-started-print}

1. Wait for the pages to show. A short message shows while they are made.
2. Press **Download PDF** to save the file on this device.
3. Press **Print** to print the document.
4. Press **Close** when you have finished.

## Using This Guide {#getting-started-user-guide}

This guide has only the parts your role uses. Someone with another role reads other parts.

![The User Guide page](shot:getting-started-guide-page)

### Open the guide {#getting-started-guide-open}

1. Open the account menu.
2. Press **User Guide**. The guide opens on its own page.
3. On a desktop, the contents are at the left. Press a chapter to see its sections, then press a section.
4. On a phone, use the **Chapter** picker above the text. Then use the section picker to jump to a section.

### Search the guide {#getting-started-guide-search}

1. Type at least two letters in **Search the guide**.
2. Read the results. Each shows where it is, and the step number when it is a step.
3. Press a result. The guide opens at that step.

![The guide search with its results](shot:getting-started-guide-search)

### Open the guide beside your work {#getting-started-guide-panel}

On a desktop you can read the guide without leaving the page you are on.

1. Hold `Ctrl` and press `H`. A panel slides in from the right.
2. The panel opens at the part of the guide for the page you are on. If there is none, it opens at the contents.
3. Use **Search the guide** in the panel, or press a section in the contents.
4. Press **Open in Page** to read it on the full page.
5. Press `Esc`, press the close button, or hold `Ctrl` and press `H` again to close the panel.

![The guide panel beside a page](shot:getting-started-guide-panel)

> **Tip:** The panel is not on a phone, which has no keyboard. Use **User Guide** in the account menu instead.

### Go back to the guide {#getting-started-guide-back}

Some steps name a screen, such as [Current Ratings](#ratings/current). Press it to open that screen.

1. Press the name of the screen in the step.
2. The screen opens. A chip with **Back to Guide** stays at the bottom of the screen.
3. When you have seen the screen, press **Back to Guide**. You return to the same step.

### Download the guide as a PDF book {#getting-started-guide-book}

1. Open the guide page.
2. Press **Download PDF** at the top right.
3. Wait while the book is made. It opens in the preview. See [Previewing and Printing](#getting-started-pdf-preview).
4. Press **Download PDF** in the preview to save it.

The book has a cover, a table of contents with page numbers, and every chapter you may read, with its pictures.
